# BuildStock POS & Inventory (MVP Prototype)

An interactive stock management and counter POS prototype built for a construction materials shop.

## Features
- **Dashboard & Low-Stock Alerts:** Live KPIs, reorder queue, and one-tap restock loop.
- **Fast Counter POS:** Spec/SKU search, fractional quantities (kg, meters), contractor price overrides, and Paid / Partial / Unpaid credit sales with printable receipts.
- **Inventory & Audit Trail:** Full movement ledger (`stock_in`, `stock_out`, `adjustment`) and damage/shrinkage logging.
- **Batch Supplier Stock-In:** Multi-item restocking with cost-change tracking.
